Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Everest is an exam preparation app for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Everest you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account.
  • Profile details you choose to enter, such as your name, email, phone number and target exam.
  • Study activity. Your mock test attempts and scores, study sessions, goals, flashcard and mistake-notebook progress, and reminder settings.
  • Leaderboard entries. If you choose to publish a score to the leaderboard, your display name, exam category and score are visible to other Everest users.
  • Notification token. A device identifier used to send you exam alerts and new mock test notifications.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your progress saved and synced between sessions.
  • To show your results, analytics, goals and leaderboard standing.
  • To send reminders and exam notifications you have allowed.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

Services we use

Everest uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging) and Google Sign-In to provide sign-in, cloud storage of your progress and notifications. These services process data on our behalf under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not show ads and we do not sell your personal information.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet and network state to sign in, sync and load updates.
  • Notifications to deliver reminders and exam alerts.
  • Vibration for timer and reminder alerts.
  • Run at startup so your reminders keep working after you restart your phone.

Data stored on your phone

Some practice content and your progress are stored on your device so the app can work offline. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

How long we keep data and how to delete it

We keep your account and progress data until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your account and the data linked to it, email everestapp.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. You can also ask for a copy of your data or a correction to it at the same address.

Children

Everest is intended for exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard services to protect your data in transit and at rest. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
