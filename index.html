What’s inside

Everything you need between your first topic and your last revision.

Practice

  • Full-length and sectional mock testsTimed tests with negative marking, saved progress and detailed results.
  • OMR bubble-sheet simulatorPractise filling the sheet the way the real exam does.
  • Cut-off predictorSee how your score compares with past cut-offs.
  • Mistake notebookEvery wrong answer collected in one place for revision.
  • Mains answer writingPractice prompts to build your answer-writing habit.

Learn and revise

  • Current affairs digestsMonthly capsules with quiz questions.
  • Static GK and polity explorerBrowse the Constitution and core concepts by topic.
  • Modern history timelineEras and milestones with a chronology game.
  • River and mountain atlas, map quizLearn geography on the map, then test yourself.
  • FlashcardsQuick topic cards you can rate by confidence.

Stay on track

  • Focus timerPomodoro sessions logged to your study time.
  • Daily goals and weekly chartSee how much you studied and what is left today.
  • RemindersMorning mock drills and evening goal nudges.
  • Exam date alertsNotices for exam dates and new mock tests.
  • LeaderboardCompare progress with other aspirants.

Install in three steps

Everest is shared as an APK file, so you install it directly from this page.

  1. Download the APKTap the button below and save the file to your phone.
  2. Open the fileIf Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
  3. Open EverestSign up, pick your target exam and start your first mock test.

Because Everest is not on the Play Store yet, Android or Play Protect may show a warning before installing. Choose “Install anyway” if you downloaded the file from this page.
